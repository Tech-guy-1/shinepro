About Shine Pro

A Blantyre cleaning company built on clear process

We started Shine Pro to give offices, households and commercial premises in Blantyre a cleaning service they could set a schedule by — properly briefed, properly staffed, and properly followed through.

Our Story

Cleaning, done the way it should be

Shine Pro was founded in Blantyre to fill a simple gap: a cleaning provider that treats offices, homes and commercial premises with the same level of professionalism, regardless of the size of the job.

Every engagement starts with understanding the space and agreeing a scope of work, so clients know exactly what's included before our team arrives. That approach now shapes how we run regular contracts, one-off deep cleans, and everything in between.

What Guides Us

Our values
