Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Shine Pro collects through this website and how it is used.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Shine Pro ("Shine Pro", "we", "us") operates this website (www.shinepromw.com) to provide information about our cleaning services and to allow visitors to request quotes. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store and protect information submitted through the site.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you choose to provide to us, including:

  • Contact details such as your name, phone number and email address
  • Information about the property or service you enquire about (for example, property type, service required, and preferred dates)
  • Any other information you include in a message to us, by form, phone or email

We do not knowingly collect payment card details through this website. If a booking involves payment, arrangements are confirmed directly with our team.

3. How We Use Information

Information submitted through the contact form or by other means is used to:

  • Respond to quote requests and enquiries
  • Schedule and carry out agreed cleaning services
  • Maintain records of past and current clients for service continuity
  • Improve our services and how we communicate with clients

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies and Website Analytics

This website may use basic cookies or similar technologies to support core functionality and to understand, in general terms, how visitors use the site. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect some website features.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information with staff or subcontractors directly involved in delivering a requested service, and where required by law or to protect our legal rights. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information you provide to us against loss, misuse or unauthorised access. No method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and client information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, and meet legal or accounting obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

8. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop using it for a particular purpose, by contacting us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults enquiring about cleaning services and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will indicate when it was last revised.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled can be sent to us via our Contact page.

This policy is provided as a general template for Shine Pro's website and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional to confirm it meets applicable data protection requirements in Malawi before relying on it for compliance purposes.
