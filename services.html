What We Offer

Cleaning services for every kind of space

Six service types covering offices, homes and commercial premises across Blantyre. Each visit starts with an agreed scope, so there's no guesswork about what's included.

Service 01

Regular Cleaning

Ongoing, scheduled cleaning — daily, weekly or monthly — that keeps offices, homes and shared spaces consistently tidy without you having to think about it.

  • Dusting, surface cleaning and floor care
  • Kitchen, washroom and common-area upkeep
  • A schedule set to match your routine
Service 02

Deep Cleaning

A thorough clean that goes beyond routine upkeep — grout, skirting, behind and under furniture, fixtures and fittings — recommended periodically or ahead of an important date.

  • Detailed attention to hard-to-reach areas
  • Kitchen appliances, cabinets and fixtures
  • A strong reset point before regular cleaning resumes
Service 03

Move In / Move Out

A full clean of an empty property — for tenants handing back a rental, landlords preparing for new occupants, or a household settling into a new home.

  • Every room cleaned from empty to move-in ready
  • Cupboards, wardrobes and storage areas included
  • Timed around your handover or moving date
Service 04

After-Hours Cleaning

Cleaning scheduled before opening, after closing, or overnight — so offices and commercial premises are cleaned without interrupting staff or customers.

  • Scheduled around your operating hours
  • Site access arrangements agreed in advance
  • Suited to offices, shops and commercial sites
Service 05

Post-Construction Cleaning

Removal of dust, debris, paint residue and construction leftovers after a building or renovation project, so the space is ready for handover or occupation.

  • Dust and debris removal from all surfaces
  • Windows, floors and fixtures cleared of residue
  • Coordinated with your contractor's timeline
Service 06

Custom Cleaning

Not every job fits a standard package. Tell us about your space and requirements, and we'll put together a plan and quote around it.

  • Scope built around your exact request
  • One-off jobs or irregular schedules welcome
  • Straightforward quote once we understand the job
